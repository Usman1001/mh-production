Who We Are

MH Production & Entertainment is the production partner international crews call before they land — the fixer of logistics, the manager of locations, the reason a shoot day runs on time.

Based in Sri Lanka and built for the demands of international film-making, we handle the full operational spectrum of production: location scouting, filming permits, budgeting, art department, studio spaces, aerial & underwater camera units, casting, wardrobe, hair & makeup, and complete island-wide logistics. Bollywood, Hollywood, and Arabic productions have relied on our team to turn vision into reality without friction.
