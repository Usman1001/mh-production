Who We Are

MH Production & Entertainment is the production partner international crews call before they land — the fixer of logistics, the coordinator of ground, the reason a shoot day runs on time.

Based in Sri Lanka and built for the demands of international film-making, we handle the operational backbone of a production: shipping and freight coordination, equipment transportation, ground transportation, travel coordination and crew support, alongside full production coordination and festival operations. Bollywood, Hollywood and Arabic productions have relied on our team to move people, cargo and schedules across borders without friction.
