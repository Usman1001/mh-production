Our Story

Built on-set, not in a boardroom

MH Production & Entertainment grew out of years spent on the ground with visiting film crews — coordinating traffic at festival premieres, managing guest logistics at media summits, and running ground and travel operations for major award shows. That hands-on experience became the foundation of a dedicated production support company.

Today we provide production coordination, shipping and logistics, equipment and ground transportation, travel management and crew support to international productions filming in and moving through Sri Lanka. Our role is simple to describe and difficult to execute well: make sure nothing about the logistics gets in the way of the film.

We have supported Bollywood features, Hollywood productions and Arabic-language films, working alongside international line producers, production managers and studio logistics teams who need a partner that understands both the paperwork and the pace of a production.

Our Mission

Remove friction from international production

To deliver dependable, professional production coordination and logistics support that lets visiting productions focus entirely on the work in front of the camera — while we manage everything behind it.

Our Vision

Sri Lanka as a trusted production hub

To be recognised internationally as the production support partner of choice in Sri Lanka and the wider region — connecting global productions to reliable, on-the-ground expertise.
